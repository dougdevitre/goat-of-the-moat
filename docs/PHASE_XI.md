# Phase XI — Executive Reporting

The static dashboard includes an executive summary, a 12-dimension comparison table, and a printable evidence appendix.

## Workflow
1. Open the full or quick evaluation, or load a synthetic sample.
2. Record scores with confidence and evidence references.
3. Review the **One-page executive summary**, **Dimension comparison**, and **Evidence appendix**.
4. Select **Print executive report / Save PDF** and use the browser's print dialog.

Print styles target US Letter and separate the summary and evidence appendix into sections. Output depends on the browser and printer; the one-page summary is designed for one page but may overflow with accessibility zoom or different fonts.

## Interpretation
Recorded points are the sum of rated question points out of the 1,000-point full instrument. Coverage is the percentage of available points with ratings and evidence references. Observed performance is earned points divided by covered points. It must not be interpreted as a comparable full-instrument score when coverage is incomplete.

The quick assessment remains a non-comparable 12-question snapshot. Evidence references are user-entered labels and are not verified. This is not an award certification.
